# GATE 2026 DA — Data Science & Artificial Intelligence

Official: https://gate2026.iitg.ac.in/doc/GATE2026_Syllabus/DA_2026_Syllabus.pdf  
This is the **DA** paper (often called DS). Not CSE.

- Probability and Statistics (axioms, Bayes, distributions, CLT, tests)
- Linear Algebra (spaces, Gaussian elimination, eigen, SVD)
- Calculus and Optimization
- Programming, DS and Algorithms (Python, search/sort, graphs)
- Database Management and Warehousing
- Machine Learning (supervised + unsupervised)
- AI (search, logic, uncertainty)

Overlap already in `src/panini/gate/` (Bayes, Gauss, search/sort, graphs, SQL select). PSpice is **not** a DA topic; it is EC/IN circuits.
