# GATE 2026 IN — Instrumentation Engineering

Official: https://gate2026.iitg.ac.in/doc/GATE2026_Syllabus/IN_2026_Syllabus.pdf

1. Engineering Mathematics (incl. numerical methods)  
2. Electricity and Magnetism  
3. **Electrical Circuits and Machines** — KCL/KVL, mesh/nodal, Thevenin/Norton, RLC transients, 2-port, machines  
4. Signals and Systems  
5. Control Systems (PID, state-space)  
6. Analog Electronics (instrumentation amp, PLL)  
7. Digital Electronics (ADC/DAC, PLC)  
8. Measurements (bridges, DMM, CRO)  
9. Sensors and Industrial Instrumentation  
10. Communication and Optical Instrumentation  

PSpice is the lab tool for §3/§6. Implementation: `src/panini/pspice.pni`.
