## Architectural thesis

A cycler is a programmable lifecycle definition that takes intent through progressively specified, reviewed, verified, realized, and preserved states.

The architecture explicitly rejects the reduction:

`cycler = prompt chain`

and instead treats the cycler as an executable system containing:

- state;
- transitions;
- gates;
- conditions;
- iteration;
- dependencies;
- artifacts;
- deliverables;
- evidence;
- provenance;
- configuration/version state;
- human authorization;
- realization;
- feedback;
- continuation.

The primary direction is **thought before artifact**. A person or agent should be able to elicit, capture, structure, review, revise, and solidify a conception before realization is forced.

## Artifact-first semantics

Prompts are operations over artifacts, not the terminal object of the system.

Artifacts may be:

- text;
- code;
- data;
- documents;
- images;
- audio;
- video;
- models;
- immersive assets;
- embodied/cyberphysical outputs;
- memory records;
- manifests and packages.

Deliverables are typed lifecycle objects with status, version, provenance, dependencies, and release state.

MIME participates in artifact semantics; where ordinary MIME is insufficient, profiled MIME semantics can carry domain-specific contracts.

## Interoperability invariant

**Shared interoperability ≠ shared workflow.**

Independent cyclers retain:

- their own identity;
- state model;
- lifecycle;
- invariants;
- failure modes;
- artifact model;
- gates;
- evidence requirements.

The common Cycler Substrate supplies interoperability without imposing workflow homogenization.

## Resolution

One lifecycle can operate at different resolutions:

- **EASY** — concept, outline, minimal artifact, concise realization path.
- **MID** — structured specification, relationships, dependencies, verification criteria.
- **PRO** — formal structures, schemas, tests, proof obligations, provenance, release packages.

Resolution changes operating depth, not the underlying lifecycle identity.

## Execution, evidence and epistemics

The execution architecture separates deterministic lifecycle orchestration from probabilistic AI operations.

The infrastructure is provider-neutral: models, APIs, tools, local runtimes, edge/cloud systems, simulators, robotics, and future substrates are implementation components rather than the semantic definition of a cycler.

Consequential transitions can require explicit human review, approval, consent, or release authorization.

Claims and releases require the evidence specified by the relevant cycler contract.

Simulation and experiment retain distinct epistemic status.

Research cycles include discovery, specification, formalization, implementation, verification, simulation, experiment, falsification, documentation, audit, and refinement.

Epistemic status is explicit rather than flattened; canonical outputs must not silently lose uncertainty or provenance.

## FAKIR

FAKIR is the **domainal guide**, not a flat classification engine.

Core rule:

**RETRIEVE, DON'T RECONSTRUCT.**

FAKIR retrieves and orients domainal reality rather than assigning an identity.

Its principal coordinate space is:

`FAKIR → DOMAIN / ACTIVITY / LIVELIHOOD`

with source coordinates including:

- **ISIC** — economic activity domains;
- **ISCO** — occupation/work domains;
- **ISCED** — education/learning domains;
- **other authoritative erudition** beyond those coordinate systems.

The important distinction is: retrieve **domains**, not classifications as identities.

FAKIR is orthogonal to:

- **PEDLER** — transition/agency dynamics;
- **ILM** — language/representational independence;
- **CEM** — substrate realization;
- **Cycler** — execution/orchestration;
- **Kitab** — persistent artefact;
- **Zistgah** — ecosystem.

FAKIR's constitutional walk is:

`FAKIR → formation of Epistemological Dukedom → Abdication → post-identity mission → domain(s) → appropriate Varzish → Mahajanapad`

Abdication suspends active exercise; it does not erase the underlying sovereignty.

After abdication, the guiding question can move from identity to mission. A mission may occupy a **composite of domains**.

FAKIR guides toward an appropriate **Varzish**, which is itself retrieved rather than reconstructed. Varzish is the developmental/practice environment in which capability is developed and evidenced through practice, consequence, reflection, feedback, correction, reattempt, sustained performance, and artifacts.

## Charbagh

**Charbagh** is the Humanesque triage and wayfinding cycler.

Its constitutional purpose is not to decide identity, but to help a participant discover where and how they may participate.

Canonical cycle:

`ARRIVE → ELICIT → RETRIEVE → MAP → NAVIGATE → COMPOSE → DERIVE → ENTER → REMEMBER`

Charbagh can:

- route into an existing estate;
- permit composition across compatible estates;
- initiate derivation of a new estate where existing configurations do not suffice;
- invoke GENIE when the destination/configuration cannot yet be conceptualized.

Canonical estate examples represented in the corpus include:

- Humanesque;
- Zistgah;
- Kaivalyik;
- Cosmopolis.

**Bazmi** is the canonical derivative-estate example: a new configuration may be constituted from plural inheritances when an existing estate cannot truthfully express the configuration. Retrieval precedes interpretation and human-reviewed constitution.

The distinction is:

**FAKIR retrieves. Charbagh routes. GENIE conceives.**

## GENIE

GENIE is the **meta-cycler / creative-agency layer**.

It brings agency to Synthetiforms by allowing them to conceptualize and drive entire deliverables rather than merely execute predetermined local steps.

Ordinary cyclers should suffice when the path is already clear.

GENIE is invoked when a person or system cannot conceptualize:

- the destination;
- the deliverable;
- the required configuration;
- or the cross-cycler composition needed to realize it.

GENIE capabilities include:

- conceptualize;
- drive;
- orchestrate;
- create;
- evaluate;
- realize;
- manage deliverables;
- compose cycles;
- move across modalities;
- operate when destination is unclear.

The creative cognition loop includes:

`GENERATIVE FIELD → CORRESPONDENCE → ABSTRACTION → INTEGRATION → CRYSTALLIZATION → REALIZATION → OBSERVATION → MEMORY → RE-EXPANSION`

GENIE maintains persistent creative state so trajectories can resume across sessions and modalities.

Meta-operations are native:

- **cycler → GENIE elevation** — any cycler may elevate itself and thereby enlarge its ontology/agency while preserving identity, state, and provenance;
- **GENIE → one/many cycler categories descent** — GENIE can specialize into focused execution;
- **GENIE split** — one GENIE becomes multiple GENIEs with shared ancestry and configurable memory/autonomy;
- **GENIE merge** — multiple GENIEs become a higher-order GENIE with reconciled state and aggregated knowledge;
- **recursive meta-cycling** — GENIE can operate on itself, other GENIEs, or composite cycles;
- **composite cycles** — GENIE can compose independent cyclers into a super-cycle without collapsing their contracts.

This is the mechanism by which the ontology can expand while retaining heterogeneous underlying infrastructure.

## Heterogeneous infrastructure

Semantic lifecycle identity must remain separate from implementation substrate.

The same semantic lifecycle can be realized across:

- local compute;
- edge;
- cloud;
- simulators;
- robotics;
- other heterogeneous or future substrates.

Architecture owns meaning, state, provenance, lifecycle semantics, and contracts.

Infrastructure is implementation.

2. Do not collapse independent cyclers into one workflow.
3. Do not silently replace artifacts with prose descriptions.
4. Preserve provenance and version state.
5. Preserve epistemic status.
6. Do not treat simulation as experiment.
7. Do not treat AI provider identity as architectural identity.
8. Do not make FAKIR a classifier or identity assignment engine.
9. Preserve **retrieve, don't reconstruct**.

11. Do not make GENIE a stateless generator.
12. Preserve GENIE elevation, descent, split, merge, composition, and recursion.
13. Preserve domain composition.
14. Preserve estate plurality and derivation.
