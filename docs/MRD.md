Two documents and the data they are generated from.

**Regenerate rather than edit by hand.** Both documents are produced from the two JSON files.

## Every entity carries a provenance class

`[RETRIEVED]` corroborated in the durable record or a live repository ·
`[PARTIAL]` named and partly recorded · `[SYNTHESIS]` present only in a prior assistant's
synthesis, **not evidence** · `[UNRESOLVED]` the record does not contain it, and nothing has been
substituted.

This exists because of a specific failure: an assistant reconstructs the estate, the
reconstruction becomes a poster, the poster is later read back as a source, and a new synthesis is
built on it. Recursive self-sourcing. Retrieved fact, prior synthesis, poster-derived inference
and unresolved item are therefore kept apart.

## What is deliberately unresolved

Where the record does not contain something, it says so. A component that is mostly `[UNRESOLVED]`
is a finding, not a gap in the writing — it names the retrieval that is owed before anything can
be built.

## Before publishing anything from here

A **DOI is a dated public disclosure**. India (§3(k)) and the EPO have no general grace period.
Nothing here contains an unfiled patent claim, and `seed_mrd.sh` refuses to mint if it finds the
language of one.
